C:\> DOS games

The DOS app runs FreeDOS in an emulated PC (v86). Each game gets a folder on drive C:; at the C:\> prompt, type its name to play. Only use games you may copy: shareware, freeware, or your own.

Opens the DOS PC in a window of its own, where clicking the game locks the mouse to it (keep the pointer inside while you allow it; Esc releases it). For mouse-look games such as Quake. The PC's Desktop > DOS works too, but there the mouse stops at the window's edges.
